Story formula

  1. Choose a main character.
  2. Make the first anchor do an action.
  3. Crash it into the next anchor.
  4. Add color, sound, size, or emotion.
  5. Reverse every anchor back to its number.

Example: 10–33–09 → A soccer No. 10 flies to France and cuts a giant baguette with a knife.
